# A Map of Creative Technology

A directory and research tool for creative technology, experience design and design-technology work around the world.

Creative technology can mean a wall that moves when you walk past it, code used as a paintbrush, or a building turned into a screen. The people, places and opportunities behind that work are scattered across many websites. This resource brings them together in one catalogue.

## What's in it

| Section | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| **Studios & Companies** | Who makes the work: studios, companies, collectives, labs, institutions, funders, and community and event organisers |
| **Programmes** | Where to study it, from degrees to short courses and summer schools |
| **Jobs** | Who's hiring |
| **Open Calls** | Commissions, residencies, grants, fellowships and competitions |
| **Publications & Resources** | What to read, watch, listen to and join |
| **Tools** | What to make it with |
| **Events** | Where the field meets: festivals, conferences, exhibitions and workshops |

Entries are tagged by discipline, including experience design, creative coding, interaction design, AR/VR and spatial work, installation, projection mapping, generative art, physical computing, game design, data visualisation and AI.

## Seen from where you are

Browse the whole field worldwide, or choose a country or region. A place view shows what is based there, along with things elsewhere that are open to applicants from there, such as through remote work, worldwide eligibility, visa sponsorship or scholarships. Where eligibility isn't clear, the entry says so.

## Add to it

Anyone can browse. Sign in to save entries and to add your own by pasting a link. What you add stays private unless you suggest it to the public Map, and suggestions are reviewed before they appear.

## About the information

Entries link to their original sources. Deadlines, eligibility and other details can change, so check the source before you apply. Unknown details are left unknown, and costs and salaries aren't listed.
